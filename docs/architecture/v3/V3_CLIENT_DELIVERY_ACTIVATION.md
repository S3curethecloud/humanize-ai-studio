## Purpose

Activate architecture planning for Humanize V3 Client Delivery & Handoff.

Humanize V3 exists to transform approved authoritative project evidence into
governed client-facing delivery experiences while preserving truth,
provenance, classification, approval state, tenant boundaries, and export
authority.

Humanize V3 does not become:

- a generic AI platform management system;
- a second NEXUS;
- a source of engagement truth;
- a NEXUS database consumer;
- a NEXUS repository scraper;
- a NEXUS state writer;
- a NEXUS runtime dependency.
## Core authority model
```text
NEXUS
  authoritative engagement truth
  architecture decisions
  approved evidence
  evaluations

  deployment and operational state
  risks and approvals
          |
          | future canonical NDEB
          v
Humanize V3
  compatibility validation
  provenance preservation
  classification enforcement
  governed composition
  audience adaptation
  human approval
  export authorization
  client-facing delivery
```

NEXUS owns truth.

Humanize owns optional advanced presentation and delivery.

Presentation never becomes evidence authority.

## Activation doctrine

V3 adopts the following doctrine:

1. NEXUS determines authoritative truth.
2. Humanize determines presentation and advanced delivery experience.
3. Evidence comes before presentation.
4. NDEB consumption is read-only.
5. Provenance must survive every authorized transformation.
6. Humanize must not weaken source classification.
7. Human approval precedes client release.
8. Artifact generation authority and export/release authority are distinct.
9. V3 must not become a generic AI platform.
10. V3 must not become a NEXUS runtime dependency.
11. NDEB schema definition and deterministic generation remain NEXUS-owned.
## NDEB interlock

Humanize may plan a consumer compatibility contract.

Humanize may not define the authoritative NDEB schema.

Humanize may not generate authoritative NDEB artifacts.

A live NEXUS adapter remains blocked until NEXUS has canonically activated and
published the approved exchange schema required by the existing Humanize
adapter contract.

Humanize-side compatibility planning must therefore distinguish:

```text
consumer planning authority
!=
live adapter implementation authority
```
