# Humanize AI V3 — Client Delivery Capability Reservation

## Status

Reserved future capability.

No implementation authorized.

---

## Purpose

Reserve future enterprise client-delivery and handoff capabilities.

Humanize V3 extends Humanize as a governed enterprise AI content and delivery
experience.

Humanize V3 does not become a generic AI platform management system.

It consumes authoritative evidence from systems such as NEXUS and transforms
approved project truth into governed client-facing artifacts.

---

## Strategic Boundary

NEXUS:

"How do we architect and operate enterprise AI systems?"

Humanize:

"How do we safely communicate, govern, and deliver the results of those
systems?"

---

## Reserved Capabilities

Future Humanize V3 capabilities may include:

- NEXUS Delivery Evidence Bundle import;
- client delivery workspace;
- provenance-aware content generation;
- evidence-backed documentation;
- Claim Lock integration;
- executive presentations;
- technical documentation;
- security documentation;
- operational documentation;
- DOCX generation;
- PDF generation;
- PPTX generation;
- complete client handoff packages;
- delivery manifests;
- approval workflows;
- classification-aware export.

---

- governed rewriting;
- enterprise workspace authorization;
- Voice DNA;
- Documents;
- Audit;
- Analytics;
- Workspace;
- Quotas.

Humanize V2.11 remains reserved for:

1. Members & Roles;
2. Claim Lock administration.

Client Delivery capabilities belong to future V3 planning.

---

## Evidence First Principle

Humanize V3 must follow:

Evidence first.

Presentation second.

Generated artifacts must be traceable to authoritative sources.

---

## Security Principles

Future client delivery must preserve:

- tenant isolation;
- classification;
- provenance;
- authorization;
- approval state;
- export controls.

Humanize may strengthen presentation controls.

Humanize must not weaken source authority.
